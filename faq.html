Help Center

1. Is PlayingHours free to use?

Absolutely. PlayingHours is a free-to-play platform. We generate revenue through non-intrusive advertisements to keep the servers running and bring more games to you.

2. My game is lagging, what should I do?

Game lag usually happens due to a slow internet connection or low browser memory. Try clearing your browser cache, closing other tabs, or restarting your browser. Using Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge is recommended.

3. How do I save my game progress?

Most of our games use local storage to save progress. However, to ensure your scores are saved permanently on our leaderboards, we recommend you create a free account via our Login page.

4. Can I play these games on mobile?

Yes! PlayingHours is fully responsive. You can enjoy almost all our games on Android and iOS devices without downloading any app. Just open the site in your mobile browser.

5. Found a bug or inappropriate behavior?

We maintain a strict community policy. If you encounter a bug or a player using cheats/hacks, please use the 'Contact' page to report it. Our moderators review all reports within 24 hours.
