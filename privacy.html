Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At PlayingHours, accessible from playinghours.vercel.app, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by PlayingHours and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

If you register for an account, we may ask for your contact information, including items such as username, age, and gender. If you play as a guest, we only use a temporary nickname chosen by you.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to operate and maintain our website, improve user experience, and track game leaderboards and coin balances.

3. Log Files & Cookies

PlayingHours follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. Like any other website, PlayingHours uses 'cookies' to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited.

4. Advertising Partners

Some of the advertisers on our site may use cookies and web beacons. Our advertising partner is Adsterra. Each of our advertising partners has its own Privacy Policy for its policies on user data.
